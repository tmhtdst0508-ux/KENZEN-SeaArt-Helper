■About Wildcards for Dynamic Prompts

These are wildcards that can be used when installing the Dynamic Prompts extension in a local Stable Diffusion environment.

(Your Stable Diffusion folder)\extensions\sd-dynamic-prompts\wildcards

Put them in this folder and specify “__filename__” in your prompt.

■File Structure

hair_set_en: Parent file for specifying the entire hair set
    hair_length_en: Hair length
    hair_bangs_en: Bangs
    hair_tying_en: Hair ties
    hair_color_en: Hair color
    hair_color_en: Hair color
eye__color_en: Eye color
expressions_en: Facial expressions
angle_en: Angle
sexual_positions_en: Positions
